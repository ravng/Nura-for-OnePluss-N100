PROVEN HARDWARE
---------------
- Graphical login and Phosh desktop with Pixman rendering.
- Display and internal storage.
- USB Ethernet and SSH.
- Volume-up, volume-down, and power/wake buttons.
- Touch through the billie2-touch-resume workaround.
- Internal speaker through direct ALSA, PulseAudio, and OS sound settings;
  automatic startup is enabled through the billie2-audio OpenRC boot service.
- Partial accelerated GPU path: Ubuntu Touch Halium/libhybris/Mir initializes
  Adreno 610/HWC2 and displayed a Wayland client, but is not the normal login.
- Native Wi-Fi automatically starts through the billie2-wifi default-runlevel
  OpenRC service. The service launches its bounded 120-second worker after the
  audio foundation and NetworkManager without blocking boot. Patched tqftpserv
  exposes service 69, wifi/wlfw-bdf.c sends BDF/calibration, FW_READY arrives,
  and wlan0 registers. NetworkManager automatically reconnects, receives DHCP,
  and passes traffic. Stock gIPAConfig=0x7d froze association; the startup
  script writes runtime gIPAConfig=0 while retaining IPA firmware for SMMU.
- Partial modem/IPA foundation. SIM registration and calls remain unproven.

AUDIO BREAKTHROUGH
------------------
The physical WCD937x codec and speaker work. The critical invariant is Android's
boot ordering, not a DTB/DTBO or kernel difference:

1. Expose every modem_a/image firmware file, including adsp2.* and *.jsn, under
   the directory named by /sys/module/firmware_class/parameters/path.
2. Load the PDR, notifier, snd-event, APR, and ADSP-loader foundation modules.
3. Start qrtr-ns and explicit pd-mapper with all service-map JSON files. Starting
   these before the notifier foundation can miss the required service state.
4. Load the remaining downstream audio modules in Android init.target.rc order.
   audio_wcd937x must precede audio_wcd937x_slave; audio_machine_bengal is last.
5. Only after all modules are loaded, write 1 to /sys/kernel/boot_adsp/boot,
   matching Android init.qcom.rc early-boot behavior.
6. Re-probe soc:qcom,msm-audio-apr:qcom,q6core-audio after ADSP is online.
7. Apply the Android mixer_paths_qrd.xml speaker route.

Booting ADSP before the full module stack caused the WCD937x RX SoundWire slave
to remain unenumerated with "SWR bus clsh detected" and "device ... is not
ready". A temporary WCD937x ASoC aux stub could register a card but could never
produce sound because the physical RDAC path was absent. The stub has therefore
been removed from audio/billie2-btfm-stub.c; that module now supplies only the
missing Q6 platform device and unused BTFM DAIs.

With the corrected order:
- wcd937x-slave.a01170223 and wcd937x-slave.a01170224 both bind;
- bengal-qrd-snd-card registers;
- EAR_RDAC, AUX_RDAC, and RDAC3_MUX controls exist;
- both SIA81xx amplifiers activate;
- a 440 Hz hw:0,0 ALSA tone was physically heard;
- PulseAudio automatically replaced auto_null/Dummy Output with
  alsa_output.platform-soc_qcom_msm-audio-apr_qcom_q6core-audio_sound.stereo-fallback;
- a 660 Hz PulseAudio tone was physically heard.

audio/billie2-audio-smoke now mounts the retained Ubuntu Touch image, exposes
firmware, starts QRTR services in the proven order, loads the complete audio
stack, boots ADSP, and applies the speaker route. audio/billie2-audio.initd runs
it as the billie2-audio OpenRC boot service. A full reboot proved automatic card
registration, both codec endpoints, the PulseAudio sink, and speaker output;
output also worked from OS sound settings. Earpiece, microphones, headphones,
route switching, and call audio are still untested.

SAFETY
------
- Do not start the complete Android LXC as a general hardware shortcut. It
  replaced Nura's USB gadget; a configfs-isolated attempt entered CrashDump.
- Do not unload partially initialized WLAN modules; this previously caused a
  kernel Oops.
- Keep WLR_RENDERER=pixman for the default greeter and Phosh session.
- Keep the known-good boot image available before any boot or DT change.
- Downloads belong under /home/ravn/storage/nuraport/downloads, not /tmp.
